# Photo Fumble

A shared game where friends guess, discuss, or bluff about personal memories using
photos, recordings, and chat excerpts.

## Language

**Room**:
A reusable space for one group, entered with a six-digit code or invite link.
Its players, host, games, and rankings are separate from other groups.

**Session**:
The gathering of players within one room sharing a host and mode selection. A session can contain
successive games, each with its own score.

**Mode**:
One of the five selectable game types: Memory Quiz, Voice Detective, Chat Archaeology,
Memory Bluff, or Memory Impostor.

**Game**:
A self-contained play of one selected mode, with its own score and final ranking.
Points do not carry into another game or mode.

**Round**:
One guessing or voting cycle within a mode, such as guessing the date of one photo.
The term does not imply a fixed sequence, numbered stages, or a change of mode.
_Avoid_: Using "round" and "mode" interchangeably.

**Host**:
The player who controls their room's session progress and chooses its next mode.
Players may select a host directly or randomly, and hosting can pass to another player.

**Uploader**:
A player who supplies a photo, recording, or chat excerpt for use in a mode.

**Submission**:
The single photo, recording, or screenshot a player contributes for a game, together
with the private answer or context needed to play it.

**Guesser**:
A player responding to another player's submitted material.

**Storyteller**:
The player presenting the true and false stories about their photo in Memory Bluff.

**Impostor**:
The player who sees a blurred version of the shared photo in Memory Impostor while
the other players see the clear version.

**Judging vote**:
A decision on whether one context explanation is close enough to the revealed truth
in Voice Detective or Chat Archaeology.
_Avoid_: Confusing this with a suspect vote identifying the impostor.

**Final ranking**:
The score-ordered result automatically shown when a game finishes. Players tied for
the highest score share the win.
_Avoid_: Overall leaderboard, cross-mode score.
